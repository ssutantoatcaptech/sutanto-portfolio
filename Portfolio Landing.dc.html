How I work

Product discovery isn't a phase — it's an ongoing practice of validating value, usability, feasibility and viability.

01 — Blueprint the system

Service blueprinting before screens. I map the whole operation — handoffs, veto points, where manual effort is protective and where it is only habit.

02 — Align the deciders

Medical directors, SRE leads, risk committees. I build the artifact that gets a room of people with veto power agreeing on one model.

03 — Ship and measure

Lean two-week cycles with real users, then a number: intake time, adoption, retention. Design-led adoption beats a mandate every time.
