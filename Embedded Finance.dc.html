Case study — Fintech

Embedded Finance: Strategic Product Discovery & User Onboarding

A top-10 U.S. bank wanted to sell banking capability as a product. I ran the discovery that turned that ambition into a validated platform roadmap — and a merchant onboarding experience its engineering partners could actually build.

client Top 10 U.S. bank — Card as a Service role Principal Product DesignerDiscovery & product strategy partners Executive leadership · Technical product owners · Merchant stakeholders stage Early-stage commercialization

Homepage concept — leading with the platform's breadth of financial services.

Alternate concept — reframing the pitch around infrastructure for the platform economy.

The problem

The vision was abstract, the constraints were not, and nobody agreed on what the product actually was.

The bank wanted external eCommerce merchants to embed banking capabilities — and wanted the same platform re-configurable for internal lines of business. Two audiences, one architecture, and no shared definition of the offer.

Merchant needs ranged from a high-growth SMB with two developers to an enterprise partner with a procurement committee. Meanwhile KYC and risk requirements were non-negotiable. Without a grounded view of the merchant lifecycle, the roadmap was a list of guesses.

Approach
01 — Test the offer with stimuli

Partnered directly with executive leadership and technical product owners, assessing product-market fit through high-fidelity design stimuli rather than slideware.

02 — Talk to the whole spectrum

Facilitated merchant discovery workshops across high-growth SMBs through established enterprise partners, including Warby Parker.

03 — Synthesize into blueprints

Turned complex banking requirements into service blueprints and user archetypes covering the merchant lifecycle from integration to success management.

04 — Hand engineering the map

Delivered end-to-end API integration blueprints from sandbox to production, giving engineering partners clarity on technical scope before commitment.

The work

Strategic onboarding: a modular journey for merchant administrators, balancing rigorous KYC requirements against a friction-free multi-persona setup.

Full onboarding walkthrough — the complete merchant setup flow, end to end.

Onboarding — condensed cut, highlighting the key decision points.

Sandbox-to-production workflow: naming the integration, inviting collaborators, selecting bundled APIs, and confirming submission — the enterprise path for enabling a new financial service.

Outcome

Pilot approved

Executive buy-in for CaaS

Sandbox → prod

API scope made concrete

New standard

Onboarding velocity for the LOB

An abstract product vision became a concrete, validated roadmap. Onboarding friction dropped by favouring intelligent recovery over re-registration, proactive identity detection removed duplicate-account overhead, and the verification pattern library set the trust standard for everything built on the platform afterwards.
