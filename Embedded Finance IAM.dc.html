Case study — Fintech

Embedded Finance: Identity & Access Management Strategy

Discovery told us what to build. This is how it was made safe to use: the identity and access framework beneath the platform, where enterprise security constraints meet a developer experience nobody wants to fight.

client Top 10 U.S. bank — Card as a Service role Lead Experience ArchitectIdentity & access strategy partners Product · Engineering · Security, Risk & Compliance stage Platform foundations — part two of two

End-to-end registration: the full architectural path from invitation through verification to privilege escalation and an active merchant state.

The problem

Security wanted every door locked. Merchants wanted to be through it in minutes. Both were right.

Discovery-led insight had to become system-level behavior. Who can act on a live financial account, how identity is proven, what happens when verification fails — each answer was owned by a different group with veto power.

The default answers were destructive: duplicate accounts when a merchant already existed, dead ends when a check failed, and administrative settings sitting a click away from money movement. High-risk constraints were non-negotiable — so the experience had to absorb them.

Approach
01 — Align the veto holders

Orchestrated cross-functional alignment between Product, Engineering and Security/Risk/Compliance leads — one shared model of who may do what, and when.

02 — Split the architecture

Architected an authentication information architecture that separates pre- and post-login capability, drawing a hard boundary around high-stakes financial operations.

03 — Detect identity early

Developed a proactive identity-detection framework so an existing merchant is recognised before registration — recovery paths in place of duplicate records.

04 — Standardize trust

Built a trust-centric UI library for verification and multi-factor tasks, so every sensitive interaction on the platform reads the same way.

The work

Identity resolution: system behavior for detecting and resolving an existing merchant account, preventing duplicate records and tightening the platform's security posture.

Security information architecture: the map of administrative boundaries, separating platform settings from high-stakes financial operations.

Core UI library: trust-centric verification and multi-factor patterns, standardized across the ecosystem.

Graceful recovery: guiding merchants through verification failures and security edge cases without compromising platform integrity.

Outcome

No duplicates

Identity detected up front

Onboarding friction removed

One trust language

Verification library adopted

Security requirements stopped being a tax on the experience. Intelligent recovery replaced destructive re-registration, proactive detection removed duplicate-account overhead before it reached operations, and the verification library became the trust standard for everything built on the platform afterwards — strengthening the security posture while keeping the merchant experience usable.
