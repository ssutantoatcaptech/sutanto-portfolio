In my words

I bring clarity to complex systems — aligning strategy, teams and execution so meaningful work actually ships.

Over the past fifteen years I've designed and delivered enterprise-scale, human-centered products across finance, healthcare, technology, logistics and eCommerce.

I work hands-on while partnering closely with product and engineering to frame the right problems, navigate ambiguity, and move from insight to impact. Much of that work sits in regulated or operationally intensive environments, where design has to balance user needs, business goals and technical reality.

Product discovery is central to how I work — not a one-time phase but an ongoing practice. I care about validating value, usability, feasibility and viability, so we aren't only building things well but building what truly matters.

More recently I've been exploring generative AI through practical experimentation: how intelligent systems can support human decision-making with care, clarity and responsibility.
