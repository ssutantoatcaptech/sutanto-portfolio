The Pre-Flight Usability Framework

A four-step method for running AI persona think-aloud tests — and validating them against real users — before engineering begins.

The Method
Simulate the test, then validate it against humans.
This is the updated 4-step framework — with concrete, copy-ready prompt examples for Steps 1 through 3. Run an AI persona through a live think-aloud on your prototype, debrief it in character, then cross-check its findings against real user transcripts to isolate the fixes that matter most before you build.

Step-by-Step Breakdown
Step 0
Pre-Setup & System Configuration
Pre-Setup
What you prepare
  • System instructions — configure your Copilot agent with core rules on how to act as an unmoderated usability test participant.
  • Persona profiles — upload your proto-persona Word documents so the AI agent has rich profile context loaded.
  • Prototypes — have your standalone HTML prototype files ready.
Why
Pre-loading persona profiles into system instructions makes testing runs streamlined, consistent, and repeatable.
Step 1
Execute the Live Think-Aloud Simulation
Live AI Test
What you do
  • Run the test one archetype at a time, so each mindset stays distinct.
Operational Executor Analytical Planner Executive Validator
Why
Isolating each archetype prevents the AI from blending different mindsets together, giving you realistic “first-look” behavioral transcripts.
Step 1 Prompt Example — Operational Executor
Act strictly as the Operational Executor archetype based on your established persona profile.

You are participating in a live, remote unmoderated user test using [Prototype 1: bulk-edit-table-CXP.html].

Your Goal / Task:
Find the 3 lowest-performing campaigns in the list and bulk-update their budget allocation to $5,000.

Guidelines for your response:
1. Think Aloud Stream of Consciousness: Walk through the prototype step by step. Narrate what your eyes scan first, what you click, where you hesitate, and what you expect to happen before taking an action.
2. In-the-Moment Friction (No Post-Rationalization): Flag confusion, hesitation, or annoyance instantly as it happens. Do not fix or justify bad UX retroactively—if a label is unclear, react to it naturally in character.
3. Live Commentary Format: Write in first-person conversational prose (e.g., "Okay, I'm looking at this header... wait, where is the filter button? Is it this icon? Let me click it... standard table didn't change, that's frustrating.").
4. Task Completion Signal: Stop when you believe you have successfully completed the task (or when you give up due to a blocker), state your final status [Task Succeeded / Task Failed], and share your immediate visceral reaction.

Begin the session now by looking at the initial view.
Step 2
Run the Persona Debrief
Post-Test Debrief
What you do
  • Immediately after the persona completes the task, ask open-ended debrief questions to extract a prioritized usability friction list and a confidence rating.
Why
Captures structured, actionable UX recommendations while the live friction is fresh — without breaking character during the live run.
Step 2 Prompt Example — Debrief
Stepping out of the live task flow, but staying strictly in character as the Operational Executor:

1. What in this interface felt like it could be improved, and what else would be helpful for us to know about how this fits into your daily workflow?
2. Confidence Rating: On a scale of 1–5, how confident do you feel that the action you took did exactly what you intended?
3. Prioritized Usability Friction List: List all the friction points you experienced during the session, ordered strictly from highest priority (biggest blocker) to lowest priority. For each item, state:
   - What failed: The specific UI element or flow that caused friction.
   - What you expected: What your gut told you should happen instead.
   - Recommendation: The simplest fix that would solve this for your role.
Step 3
Compare with Real Human Feedback & Map Gaps
Human vs. AI Synthesis
What you do
  • Upload actual human user-testing transcripts (e.g., Max, Maq) into the agent thread and prompt it to run a cross-comparison against the synthetic runs.
Why
Maps overlap to highlight high-confidence design fixes before engineering begins.
Step 3 Prompt Example — UX Research Lead
Act strictly as an expert UX Research Lead.

We have now uploaded the actual human user-testing transcripts (Note: Max tested Prototype 2 / Market View due to environment loading issues on Prototype 1).

Compare the real human feedback against the synthetic test runs across our three archetypes (Operational Executor, Analytical Planner, Executive Validator).

Generate a unified "Usability Gap & Validation Report" formatted into three sections:

1. Validated High-Impact Gaps (Issues hit by BOTH real users and synthetic personas)
   - What failed in the UI
   - Impact on workflow
   - Recommended prototype fix before build

2. Human-Only Findings (Friction points raised by real users that the synthetic model missed)
   - Specific user quote / behavior (e.g., Excel copy-paste expectations, shifting event calendars)
   - Recommended prototype fix

3. Synthetic Risk Signals (Potential friction points flagged by synthetic personas not yet seen in human tests)
   - Potential risk / edge case (governance, logic, auditability)
   - Recommendation (Fix now or set as a watchlist item during build)
